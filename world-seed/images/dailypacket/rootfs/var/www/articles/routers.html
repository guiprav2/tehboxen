Your network

What your home router knows about you

The quiet box at 192.168.1.1 hands out your address, answers your DNS and decides what gets in.

Help desk

Your router leases you an address over DHCP, remembers which machine had which address, and forwards your DNS questions upstream. Behind it, every machine on your LAN shares one public address: the router rewrites connections on their way out and keeps a table to match the replies.

Nothing comes in unless you ask: a port forward is the only way for the outside world to reach a server on your LAN. Try ip route and cat /etc/resolv.conf to see what your machine was told.
